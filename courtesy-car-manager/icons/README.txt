Courtesy Car Manager — app icons

To change the icon, replace these files with your own, keeping the same
names and exact pixel sizes (PNG):

  icon-192.png            192 x 192   Android home screen / install prompt
  icon-512.png            512 x 512   Android splash screen and app list
  icon-maskable-512.png   512 x 512   Android "shaped" icons: fill the whole square,
                                      keep the important part in the middle 60%
  apple-touch-icon.png    180 x 180   iPhone / iPad home screen: fill the whole square,
                                      no see-through parts (the phone rounds the corners)
  favicon-32.png           32 x 32    browser tab
  icon.svg                 any size   browser tab (sharp at any size) and the source design

icon.svg and icon-maskable.svg are the originals the PNGs were made from.

After publishing, installed apps pick up a new icon on their own, but
phones can take a day or so to refresh a home-screen icon. On iPhone the
quickest way is to remove the app from the home screen and add it again.
