Privacy notice

What we collect on build-tracker.co.uk, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Build Tracker Solutions (Alan Brindley), Cheshire East, UK. We decide how the personal information described below is used, which makes us the “controller” under UK data protection law.

Questions about this notice, or about your information? Email info@build-tracker.co.uk or phone 07931095631.

This notice covers the website at build-tracker.co.uk, including the chat assistant. Some of our apps also run at their own web addresses; if one of them has its own notice, that one applies there.

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it

Messages you send us

Your name, email address and whatever you tell us, when you email or phone us or use the contact form. The contact form opens a message in your own email program, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

  • Why: to reply to you and sort out your enquiry.
  • Our legal reason: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or steps you asked for before becoming a customer.
  • How long: up to 2 years after we last dealt with your enquiry.

Your account

If you create an account on the site: your email address, your password (kept in scrambled form, so we cannot read it), the date you signed up, when you sign in, and which of our tools you subscribe to.

  • Why: to run your account and give you access to what you have paid for.
  • Our legal reason: to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • How long: while your account is open. If you ask us to close it, we delete it, apart from payment records we are legally required to keep.

Payments

Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. We keep a record that you subscribed (which tool, whether it is active, and the dates), plus the payment records and receipts that our accounts need.

  • Why: to take payment, give you access, and keep proper accounts.
  • Our legal reason: contract, and our legal duty to keep accounting records.
  • How long: for as long as tax law requires, usually around 6 years.

The chat assistant

When you use the chat bubble, we save your questions and the answers. We also save a random ID your browser makes up (so one person’s questions can be grouped together) and a scrambled version of your internet address (so we can limit how many questions one person can ask; we do not keep your actual address). Your questions are sent to Google’s Gemini AI service to write the answers. The answers come from an AI, so they can be wrong: please check prices and anything important with us.

Please don’t type personal details, passwords or card numbers into the chat.

  • Why: to answer your question, stop misuse and improve the assistant.
  • Our legal reason: our legitimate interests in running and improving it.
  • How long: up to 90 days.

Technical information when you visit

Like any website, loading our pages sends your internet address and details of your browser to the companies that deliver them: our web host (GitHub) and the services that supply fonts and code libraries (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and Stripe’s payment script on the home page). We do not use this to track you or build a profile of you.

Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run visitor-tracking or analytics tools on this website. Your browser does keep a few small items so the site works:

  • a note that you closed the “get our apps” strip on the home page, so it stays closed;
  • the chat assistant’s random ID (kept until you clear your browser data) and the chat so far (kept only until you close the tab);
  • your sign-in, if you log in to your account;
  • identifiers that Stripe may set when you go to pay, to prevent fraud.

Who we share information with

We use these companies to run the website. They handle information on our behalf or, for payments, under their own rules. We do not sell your information.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and the chat assistant’s back end (privacy policy).
  • Stripe: payments (privacy policy).
  • Google: the Gemini AI service behind the chat assistant, and Google Fonts (privacy policy).
  • GitHub: hosts this website (privacy statement).

Some of these companies handle information outside the UK, including in the USA. When that happens, UK law requires safeguards to be in place, such as approved contract terms. We may also share information if the law requires us to.

If you use our software

If you use our tools to store details of your own customers or staff, you decide what goes in and how long it stays. We store it for you, only so the tools work, and we do not use it for anything of our own.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information (in some cases we must keep certain records);
  • limit how we use it, or stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • give you your information in a portable form, where that applies.

Email info@build-tracker.co.uk. It is free, and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first at info@build-tracker.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we found.

You can also complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113.

Children

Our website and software are for businesses and are not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Keeping information safe

We use well-known providers, secure (HTTPS) connections and scrambled passwords, and we only keep what we need. No online service can be made perfectly safe. If a problem affects your information, we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
