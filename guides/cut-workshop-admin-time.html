⏱️ 5 Ways to Cut Admin Time in Your Garage

Spend less time on paperwork and more time earning money — simple changes that save hours every week.

Most independent garages spend 3–5 hours per week on admin: writing job cards, creating quotes, searching for parts records, and updating customer details. That’s time you could be working on cars. Here’s how to cut that down.

1. Ditch paper job cards

Handwritten notes get smudged, lost, or hard to read. Digital job logs are faster and clearer:

  • Create a new job in 30 seconds from your phone or computer
  • Add parts used, labour time, and notes while you work
  • All records stay neat, searchable, and safe forever

2. Make quoting faster

Writing quotes from scratch takes time. Templates and saved items speed this up:

  • Save standard jobs and common parts with prices
  • Build a quote in 2 clicks instead of typing everything out
  • Send it by email or share a link — customers can approve it instantly
  • Turn approved quotes directly into work orders

3. Stop wasting time searching for parts

Good stock control cuts down on delays and re‑ordering mistakes:

  • Log what you have, where it’s stored, and when to re‑order
  • See exactly which jobs used which parts
  • Reduce over‑ordering and cut down on dead stock

4. Keep everything in one place

Stop switching between emails, folders, and notebooks. One system holds:

  • All customer and vehicle details
  • Full work history and invoices
  • Supplier contacts and purchase orders
  • Digital copies of MOTs, receipts and notes

5. Automate the boring work

Let software do the calculations and filing:

  • Auto‑add labour and parts totals
  • Update customer history automatically
  • Generate professional records for customers or HMRC
