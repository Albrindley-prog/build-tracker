    Backup

    A backup is a small file with your notes and summaries (not the audio). Keep it somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or email it to yourself. Restoring adds missing notes and never deletes anything; if a note is in both places, the more recently edited copy is kept.

    Notes and audio are stored in this browser on this computer only. Chrome and Edge send your voice to their own speech service to transcribe it, so live transcription needs an internet connection.
